NARENDRA — PERSONAL PORTFOLIO

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the portfolio_website folder.
3. Double-click index.html (Chrome or Edge recommended).

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Confirm the display name "Narendra" and education details.
- Replace hello@example.com in index.html with your real email address.
- Replace the sample project cards with links/screenshots of your actual projects.
- You can edit all text in index.html and colors/layout in style.css.

FEATURES
- Minimal black-and-white aesthetic inspired by your reference images.
- 3-second opening screen with your name.
- Responsive desktop and mobile layout.
- Bottom-left circular back-to-top button, shown after scrolling.
- Your supplied portrait is included in grayscale in assets/portrait.png.
